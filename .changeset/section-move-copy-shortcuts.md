Move and duplicate a canvas section from the keyboard

- On a focused canvas section, Alt+Arrow Up and Alt+Arrow Down move it one place, the same move as the toolbar buttons, and keep it selected and focused.
- Cmd+D (macOS) or Ctrl+D duplicates it, then selects and focuses the copy. These shortcuts replace the browser's own Cmd/Ctrl+D (bookmark) while a section has focus.
- Keys pressed inside a section's own content are left alone, as for the other section keys.
